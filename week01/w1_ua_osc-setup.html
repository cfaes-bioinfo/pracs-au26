Ungraded assignment: OSC account setup

Week 1 (deadline: Sun Aug 30, 5 pm)

Author

Jelmer Poelstra

Published

August 22, 2026

Before you start

By 5 pm on Sun Aug 30, you should make sure that you have access to the Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC), and the OSC Project for this course (PAS2880).

You will do much of your work during this course by connecting to the Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC), starting during next Monday’s class.

To access OSC, you will need to have an account. This course has its own OSC “Project”, and membership of this specific project will allow you to access our shared files and to reserve computing resources.

What you should do

  • If you already have an OSC account
  • If you don’t have an OSC account yet

  1. You should have received an email from OSC that you were added to OSC Project PAS2880. If you haven’t received this email, please contact Jelmer.

  2. Check that you can log in to OSC at https://ondemand.osc.edu using your username and password.

  1. You should have received an email from OSC that you were added to OSC Project PAS2880, with a link to create an account. Follow the instructions in the email to create your account.

    At some point during the course, you’ll have to actually type your password in a context where you won’t be able to see what you are typing. We therefore recommend a password that is not extremely complicated or long.

  2. Check that you can log in to OSC at https://ondemand.osc.edu using your username and password (it may take up to half an hour after you create your account for this to work).

If you have any questions about this or run into problems, don’t hesitate to contact Jelmer.
